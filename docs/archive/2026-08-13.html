Match-fixing scandal rocks Laos football team in Asean Cup

The Lao Football Federation has requested an immediate investigation by the ministry of Public Security's Immigration Police Department into alleged match-fixing, bribery, and illegal gambling involving members of its national team during the Asean Hyundai Cup.

Federation leadership interrogated every squad member on Aug 2 and Aug 6 following group stage matches played on July 25, July 28, Aug 1, and Aug 4. Private security firms will soon be allowed to hack overseas cybercriminals

Under a National Security Presidential Memorandum issued Thursday by President Donald Trump, the United States is preparing to enlist private security firms to carry out authorized operations, including cyberattacks, against foreign transnational criminal organizations. Developed by the National Coordination Center under the Homeland Security Task Force, the initiative will operate with oversight from the Departments of Justice and Homeland Security to target activities such as ransomware, financial fraud, phishing campaigns, sextortion schemes, and impersonation scams.

The directive authorizes private companies to "conduct Cyber Surveillance Operations and Cyber Effects Operations" against "cyber-enabled" foreign criminal groups that target American individuals, organizations, or government entities without operating under the direct control of a foreign government.
