Five dead, 41 missing after Indonesian ferry catches fire

An Indonesian ferry traveling from Surabaya in East Java to Makassar in South Sulawesi caught fire at sea on Sunday off Madura island. Five people were killed and 41 remained missing after some jumped overboard, while 225 people were rescued by search and rescue teams and a nearby ship. Four other vessels on standby were unable to approach the ferry due to the risk posed by flammable material on board.

The captain reported the fire to the ferry company before losing contact. A larger rescue operation was dispatched on a six-hour journey from Surabaya, with Ministry of Transportation officials stating that passenger and crew safety remained the primary priority during evacuation efforts.

Here's how engineers plan to save the satellite sent to save NASA's Swift mission

A refrigerator-size satellite named Link spun out of control more than 200 miles above Earth while traveling to rescue NASA's $500 million Swift gamma-ray observatory. Rotating on multiple axes, the spacecraft could not maintain a reliable communications link with the ground, which complicated efforts to arrest the spin. Two of its three reaction wheels used for pointing stopped working, while sporadic radio contact showed an issue with some of its cold gas thrusters.

Built, owned, and operated by satellite servicing startup Katalyst Space Technologies, Link was deployed under a $30 million contract from NASA. The plan required the satellite to reach the Swift observatory, grab onto it, and boost its orbit before burning up in the atmosphere from aerodynamic drag.

As Reddit stock falls, CEO questions value of Google's AI Overviews

As Reddit stock falls, Chief Executive Officer Steve Huffman has questioned the value of Google's AI Overviews feature during quarterly earnings discussions. In a letter to investors, Huffman argued that consumers are seeking real human perspective as the internet fills with synthetic content, stating that users want direct human experiences rather than automated summaries. He noted that while traditional search links previously drove growth across the web, AI Overviews has not demonstrated a similar positive impact for publishers and businesses.

Reddit currently holds a $60 million licensing deal with Google but is considering ending the agreement, according to a report from the Wall Street Journal. Several prominent publishers, including Reuters and Politico, are also considering cutting ties with Google. While a Pew Research study of 900 US adults found that AI Overviews reduced referrals to publisher sites by nearly half compared to traditional links, Google rejected the findings as flawed, stating it continues to direct billions of daily clicks to websites.

After noise complaints, judge orders Waymo to stop overnight charging in Santa Monica

A local judge has ordered autonomous vehicle company Waymo to halt overnight charging at its side-by-side facilities in Santa Monica, California. Los Angeles Superior Court Judge Bradley S.
