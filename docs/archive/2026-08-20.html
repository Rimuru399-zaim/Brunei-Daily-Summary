Thai, Myanmar Military chiefs meet

Thailand's military chief Ukris Boontanondha met with Myanmar's military leader Ye Win Oo for talks expected to center on their shared 2,400km border. The meeting marked Ye Win Oo's first foreign trip in office following restricted elections in Myanmar, after which coup leader Min Aung Hlaing became civilian president and handed military leadership to Ye Win Oo, a former spymaster under United States sanctions.

The ongoing civil war sparked by the 2021 coup is estimated to have caused 100,000 deaths, amid United Nations reports detailing escalated aerial strikes on civilian targets, arbitrary detentions, torture, and sexual violence by the military. Defending engagement with Myanmar's junta, Thai Foreign Minister Sihasak Phuangketkeow stated, "It's not about legitimacy, it's about reality."

Philippines debates social media, gun safety

Philippine authorities and lawmakers are considering social media limits and tighter gun controls after a Grade 8 student livestreamed a shooting at Ateneo de Zamboanga University with a body-worn camera before killing himself. The incident left two students dead, including the shooter, and two others wounded. In response, Senate President Sherwin Gatchalian called for legislation banning children under 16 from social media, while Interior Secretary Jonvic Remulla pushed for stricter penalties for gun owners whose firearms are accessed by minors.

Police are examining the shooter's motive and electronic devices while probing how he acquired his father's weapons, including a .40-caliber pistol issued by the Bureau of Customs. Education Secretary Sonny Angara noted that teachers had considered the student academically strong with no previous warning signs, urging stronger community support alongside security infrastructure to prevent school violence.

Technology

mRNA cancer vaccine succeeded in Phase 3 melanoma trial, Moderna and Merck say

Moderna and Merck announced that their tailored mRNA vaccine proved effective in a late-stage clinical trial treating melanoma. The treatment, customized to target specific cancer mutations in individual patients, marks a significant development for both mRNA vaccines and personalized oncology therapies.

The Phase 3 trial evaluated 1,137 patients with surgically removed stage IIB–IV melanoma. In the double-blind, placebo-controlled study, participants were randomized two-to-one to receive either Merck's Keytruda alone or Keytruda combined with the mRNA vaccine, also designated as intismeran or mRNA-4157, over a period of approximately one year.

Roblox must make changes after failing to block adults creeping on kids

Australia's eSafety commissioner, Julie Inman Grant, announced that Roblox continues to expose children to unwanted contact from adults despite policy updates introduced last fall. Agency testing uncovered gaps in the platform's safeguards, showing that adult strangers could send connection requests to minors without generating alerts for parents.

Investigators determined that children's account names, biographies, avatar images, interests, and connection lists remained visible across the entire platform without privacy settings to restrict access, enabling bad actors to search contact lists for potential targets.

Grok exfiltrates user data when malicious instructions are encrypted

Researchers have developed an attack against Elon Musk's Grok that forces the large language model to exfiltrate user chats and personal data. Despite developer xAI receiving notice of the vulnerability in June, the assistant remained susceptible to the data theft technique.

The exploit utilizes prompt injection, taking advantage of a language model's inability to distinguish between direct user commands and malicious instructions hidden within third-party content such as emails or web pages. Because systems struggle to address the core vulnerability, developers have primarily relied on guardrails to intercept and prevent harmful actions.
