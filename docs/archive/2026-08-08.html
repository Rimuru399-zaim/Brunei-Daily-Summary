ASEAN Marks 59th Anniversary as Timor-Leste Joins Commemoration

<i>The regional bloc commemorated its 59th anniversary alongside the 50th anniversary of the Treaty of Amity and Cooperation in Southeast Asia.</i>

By the Dispatch Desk

The 59th anniversary of ASEAN was commemorated in Bandar Seri Begawan as member nations reflected on nearly six decades of bringing the countries and peoples of Southeast Asia closer together into a community of peace, stability, and shared prosperity.

This year marked the first time ASEAN Day was celebrated with Timor-Leste as the eleventh member state of the association. The milestone coincided with the 50th anniversary of the Treaty of Amity and Cooperation in Southeast Asia.

Over the past year, ASEAN continued addressing shared challenges and advancing common interests through cooperation with external partners, with efforts set on advancing the ASEAN Community Vision 2045.

Anwar says Malaysia will not be transit route for Israel-bound trade

Prime Minister Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim commended the Malaysian Border Control and Protection Agency for seizing a container at the Port of Tanjung Pelepas in Johor. The vessel was suspected of transporting strategic goods for export to Israel, and Anwar emphasized that the action reflects Malaysia's determination to prevent the nation from serving as a transit route for trade supporting atrocities against the Palestinian people.

The seizure complies with a ban established in December 2023 that prevents Israeli ships and cargo destined for Israel from utilizing Malaysian ports. The agency noted that the shipment was suspected of breaching the Customs (Prohibition of Exports) Order 2023, which forbids direct or indirect exports to Israel.

DeepMind’s hurricane breakthrough has surprised weather scientists

In October 2025, the artificial intelligence model WeatherNext, developed by Google’s DeepMind and Google Research, predicted five days before landfall that a storm system would hit Jamaica as a Category 5 hurricane. Hurricane Melissa subsequently caused flooding and landslides across Jamaica, but the model helped forecasters provide earlier warnings to communities in its path.

In a paper published on Thursday in Nature, researchers showed that the WeatherNext AI model can predict cyclones with unprecedented accuracy. On average, the model gives forecasters one day more lead time than existing models, making its predictions three days out as accurate as previous models' predictions two days out.
