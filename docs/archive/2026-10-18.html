PM: Asean mechanism proposed to strengthen haze response

Malaysia has proposed an Asean-level mechanism to strengthen coordination, preparedness, and collective action against transboundary haze caused by forest and land fires, Prime Minister Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim announced. The haze has forced school closures across several states and areas in Malaysia, while fires in Indonesia have shrouded parts of Southeast Asia in toxic haze and prompted highest-level alerts.

Anwar stated that Malaysia remains ready to assist Indonesia, noting that containing the situation is challenging due to unpredictable weather conditions and the vast areas affected. Indonesia has deployed tens of thousands of personnel, aerial assets, and 2.4 tonnes of dry ice over South Sumatra in weather modification operations, while prolonged peatland and forest fires in Kalimantan have degraded air quality and caused acute water shortages.

Indonesia risks rare MSCI exclusion and loss of global capital

MSCI has warned that it may exclude Indonesia from its stock market indexes, proposing a cut to standalone status over opaque ownership, suspected coordinated trading, and inadequate enforcement. The announcement wiped about US$65 billion off the value of Indonesian equities and caused an 11.6 per cent drop on the Jakarta Composite Index, marking its largest single-day decline since the 1998 Asian financial crisis.

A final decision is due next June following a consultation that runs until next April. Banks estimate the exclusion could trigger between US$3 billion and US$6 billion in passive capital outflows alongside potential active withdrawals, while analysts warn the fallout could pressure the rupiah, raise the prospect of an interest rate hike, and complicate financing for state-linked companies in Southeast Asia's biggest economy.

Anthropic to commit 5% stake to new public benefit fund

Anthropic announced on Sunday that it will allocate 5% of its shares, subject to stockholder approval, to establish a new public benefit fund. Organized as a wholly owned limited liability company, the fund could back nonprofit donations, direct distributions to specific populations, and initiatives targeting the impact of artificial intelligence on workers. The board may also grant an additional 2% of company shares for every 50% increase in valuation beyond an undisclosed baseline. Anthropic stated that no equity held by the fund will be used for lobbying or political donations in the United States or internationally.

The Claude maker is planning an initial public offering as a public benefit corporation, a structure requiring leadership to weigh broader social interests alongside investor returns. Combined with the new fund, approximately 20% of Anthropic's shares will be dedicated to public purposes, excluding potential equity increases tied to future valuation milestones. Further information regarding the fund's governance, structure, and valuation thresholds will be released in the coming months.

Japan to launch historic mission to Mars moon

Japanese scientists are preparing to launch the unmanned Martian Moons eXploration probe to collect samples from the moons of Mars, aiming to complete the first round trip to the Martian region and shed light on the origins of the Solar System and life on Earth. The spacecraft is scheduled to launch aboard an H3 rocket from the Tanegashima Space Centre on Tuesday at 4.41am. It will reach the Martian system a year later, orbit the moon Phobos for up to three years, deploy a rover named IDEFIX to ensure a safe touchdown, and collect at least 10 grams of surface material before returning to Earth.

Conducted in collaboration with NASA, the European Space Agency, France's National Centre for Space Studies, the German Aerospace Centre, and numerous universities and technology firms, the mission will also observe the moon Deimos without landing. The Japan Aerospace Exploration Agency aims to evaluate whether Phobos can serve as a "natural space station" to establish requirements essential for eventual crewed Mars exploration.
