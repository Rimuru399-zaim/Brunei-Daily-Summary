Maju Motors unveils compact hybrid SUV, BYD ATTO 2

Maju Motors Sdn Bhd officially launched the BYD ATTO 2, introducing a sub-compact plug-in hybrid electric vehicle SUV. The public unveiling took place at the BYD Showroom in Telanai, drawing more than 200 invited guests. Powered by a 1.5-litre Xiaoyun engine paired with an electric motor, the vehicle delivers 194 horsepower and 215 Nm of torque, and accelerates from 0 to 100 km/h in 8.9 seconds.

The vehicle offers a combined driving range of up to 1,000 kilometres under WLTP standards and adopts BYD's signature Dragon Face design language. The model is available at an introductory price of BND27,900 for the first 100 units, with financing options starting from approximately BND3XX per month. A lucky draw held during the event awarded complimentary servicing packages to three attending customers.

ASEAN

NS polls: Loke, Aminuddin among biggest casualties as BN wins 18 seats

BN won 18 seats in the 36-seat Negri Sembilan state assembly, while PN secured seven seats and PH won 11 seats. Combined, BN and PN secured 25 seats, surpassing the 24-seat threshold required for a two-thirds majority. PH, which had governed Negri Sembilan since 2018, was reduced to 11 seats and lost control of the state government.

DAP secretary-general Anthony Loke lost the Chennah seat to MCA's Siow Kong Choon, while caretaker Menteri Besar Datuk Seri Aminuddin Harun lost the Linggi seat to Mohd Faizal Ramli. Four-term PH incumbent Veerapan Superamaniam also lost the Repah seat. Among BN's winners, Negri Sembilan Umno chairman Datuk Seri Jalaluddin Alias retained Pertang, while Parti Wawasan won its first-ever state seat through Ridzuan Ahmad in Gemas.

Sabah set to get 40% stake in Petronas’s floating LNG project

The Sabah state government is expected to receive a 40% stake in a floating liquefied natural gas project under an upcoming agreement with Petronas. Chief Minister Hajiji Noor stated that the state will also obtain a 20% stake in an oil and gas field in Sandakan secured by Petronas. Speaking in Kalabakan, Hajiji attributed the agreements to close cooperation between state and federal governments.

Hajiji noted that existing industrial sites, including the Kota Kinabalu Industrial Park and the Sipitang Oil and Gas Industrial Park, are nearing full capacity. As a result, Sabah is expanding industrial development to the east coast and establishing a blue economy-based industrial park in the north, while identifying about 400ha for a proposed new township in Kalabakan.

Researchers devise a full-color night vision goggle

Researchers led by Xin Tang and Ge Mu at the Beijing Institute of Technology have developed a full-color night vision device that translates infrared wavelengths into distinct parts of the visual spectrum. Human eyes normally cannot register infrared light because its photons lack the energy required to activate signaling pathways within light-sensing cells.

The technology combines mercury telluride colloidal quantum dots to absorb infrared radiation with a dual-layer OLED that converts the energy into visible color. Stacked with internal wiring, the component layers output incoming infrared light as a full-color image.
