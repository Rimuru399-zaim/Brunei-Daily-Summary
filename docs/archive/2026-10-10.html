BIBD launches landmark Sukuk to develop Brunei’s capital market

Bank Islam Brunei Darussalam has launched the BIBD Sukuk Wakalah Programme, which represents the first corporate Sukuk Wakalah issuance in Brunei Darussalam. Institutional investors will be able to take part directly in the primary issuance, while eligible retail investors can participate via BIBD Securities' Fractional Sukuk offering with a minimum investment of BND5,000.

The programme was officially launched jointly by Yang Berhormat Dato Seri Setia Dr Haji Abdul Manaf bin Haji Metussin, Coordinating Minister for Economic Policies and Minister of Economy, Trade and Industry, in his capacity as Chairman of Brunei Darussalam Central Bank, and Yang Berhormat Dato Seri Setia Awang Haji Ahmaddin bin Haji Abdul Rahman, Coordinating Minister for Social and Manpower Policies and Minister of Home Affairs, serving in his capacity as Chairman of Bank Islam Brunei Darussalam Berhad.

Baiduri Bank appoints new Group CEO

Baiduri Bank has appointed Dr Cédric Derras as its new Group Chief Executive Officer, succeeding Ti Eng Hui, who will retire on 25 October 2026 after three decades with the organization. Prosecutors drop bribery charge in Philippine VP impeachment trial

Prosecutors in Manila announced on Friday that they are dropping a bribery charge against Philippine Vice President Sara Duterte to speed up her ongoing impeachment trial. The 48-year-old continues to face charges of misuse of state funds, unexplained wealth, and an alleged assassination threat against President Ferdinand Marcos. A conviction on any charge by the Senate would remove Duterte from office and permanently bar her from elected office, halting her bid to seek the presidency in 2028.

House of Representatives prosecutors have already called witnesses and presented evidence regarding the fund misuse and assassination charges, and prosecutor Jose Manuel Diokno said the final witness concerning hidden wealth appeared Friday. Duterte will be permitted to present her defense after the prosecution completes its 60 allotted trial days. The 24-seat Senate has set the threshold for conviction at 14 votes, deciding that the constitutional two-thirds requirement of "all members of the Senate" applies only to members capable of participating, with four senators loyal to Duterte absent due to detention, hiding, or medical furlough.

Ukraine’s drones knock out AI data center belonging to "Russia’s Google"

Ukrainian drone strikes on October 8 and October 9 disabled two of the five data centers operated by Russian technology company Yandex. Located in the Sasovo and Kaluga regions of Russia, the damaged facilities have left the firm struggling to maintain its online services and artificial intelligence chatbot.

The strikes followed a Russian campaign against Ukrainian data centers that has disrupted internet and mobile phone access for thousands of people since late September. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy confirmed the operation, calling the high-precision attack a symmetrical response to Russian strikes.

NASA issues long-awaited call to industry for private space stations

NASA has issued a 360-page solicitation calling on the commercial space sector to develop private space stations to replace the International Space Station in low-Earth orbit as early as 2030. The agency stated that the initiative is intended to maintain an uninterrupted human presence in orbit for crew members carrying out exploration and science.

Commercial firms must submit their proposals to NASA by December 8. The space agency intends to choose "two or more" contractors during this stage before reaching a final decision in April.
