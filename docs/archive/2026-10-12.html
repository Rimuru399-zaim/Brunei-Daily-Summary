World Health Summit 2026: Berlin Becomes Global Meeting Point for the Future of Health

The World Health Summit 2026 gathered more than 4,000 participants from 130 countries in Berlin alongside tens of thousands online from October 11 to 13. Convening under the theme "From Crisis to Resilience: Innovating for Health," representatives from politics, science, civil society, and the private sector met to address Ebola and other infectious diseases, antibiotic resistance, pandemic preparedness, climate-related health threats, and health care delivery in conflict settings such as Ukraine. The summit also scheduled discussions with prospective candidates for the next WHO Director-General, with Summit President Axel R. Pries emphasizing that overcoming overlapping global crises requires innovation across biomedicine, financing, and political coordination.

During the official press conference, Diene Keita, Executive Director of UNFPA, announced that the Government of Bangladesh is making a 250 million euro investment in women’s health with support from the European Investment Bank. Other releases included the Wellcome Global Monitor 2026, presented by Wellcome CEO John-Arne Røttingen to document falling trust in science since the COVID-19 pandemic, and findings from the State of the World’s Emotional Health 2026 Report, shared by Gallup Managing Director Ilana Ron Levey to examine the impact of social media use on emotional well-being.

Ringgit opens higher vs US dollar on fiscal discipline pledge

The ringgit opened higher against the US dollar on Monday at 4.0820/0880, supported by the government's commitment to fiscal discipline following the tabling of the 2027 Budget. Bank Muamalat Malaysia Bhd chief economist Dr Mohd Afzanizam Abdul Rashid noted that the target to narrow the fiscal deficit to 3.3 per cent of gross domestic product in 2027 from an estimated 3.6 per cent this year should help preserve Malaysia's sovereign credit rating.

Despite external uncertainties surrounding developments in the Middle East and potential US Federal Reserve interest rate moves, the currency opened mostly higher against major and Asean currencies, gaining ground against the British pound, the euro, the Singapore dollar, the Thai baht, and the Indonesian rupiah.

Heavy rain triggers landslides, flash floods in Kundasang [WATCH]

Continuous heavy rain triggered landslides and flash floods in several areas around Kundasang, leaving an overturned car along a village road near Kampung Sinisian. Ranau district police chief Superintendent Mursalin Mahmud reported that the landslide in Kampung Sinisian covered an estimated 223 square metres, while another landslide behind Surau Al-Taqwah in Kampung Dumpiring Bawah affected roughly 74 square metres, with no casualties reported in either incident.

Flooding mixed with rocks also obstructed the main road between Kampung Pahu and Kampung Himbaan in Bundu Tuhan after an inspection revealed a blocked culvert. Eight workers from DCTF Group were sent to clear the route, supported by police personnel monitoring the area.

Bill Gates warns cyberattacks most imminent AI threat

In an interview with ABC News, Microsoft co-founder Bill Gates warned that unregulated artificial intelligence poses an increasing threat, predicting an escalation in AI-driven cyberattacks over the coming year. Gates stated that the danger of the technology "getting out of control" remains years away, emphasizing that the primary immediate concern is "bad people" deploying systems without safeguards to conduct cyberattacks and bioterrorism.

Gates also rejected the perspective advocated by US President Donald Trump that self-regulation by technology firms is sufficient to maintain safety. Pointing out that users are currently permitted to eliminate safeguards on the latest models, Gates cautioned that society is not doing enough to protect against potential harms.
