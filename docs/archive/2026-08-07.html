BDMW 2026 highlights Brunei’s vision for maritime growth

Brunei Darussalam Maritime Week 2026 officially opened on Thursday at Dermaga Diraja, bringing together government leaders, industry experts, and international partners. Organised by the Maritime and Port Authority of Brunei Darussalam, the event focuses on maritime safety, innovation, and workforce development to support sustainable economic growth. In his keynote address, Minister of Transport and Infocommunications and Chairman of MPABD Yang Berhormat Dato Seri Setia Mohd Riza bin Dato Paduka Haji Mohd Yunos stated that safety, security, and environmental protection serve as the foundation for sustainable maritime growth.

Strategic priorities outlined by the minister include modernising navigation systems through membership in the International Organization for Marine Aids to Navigation and advancing operational readiness for the Ship-to-Ship and Lay-Up Designated Area. He also noted that the expansion of Muara Port remains on track for completion in the third quarter of 2027 to more than double its annual container handling capacity, while the Port Trade Zone is expected to generate new opportunities in logistics, manufacturing, and value-added services.

MRCB-Theta Edge JV wins RM3.03bil Penang LRT systems contract [WATCH]

Mass Rapid Transit Corp Sdn Bhd awarded a RM3.03 billion system turnkey contract for the Mutiara Line Light Rail Transit project to a joint venture between Malaysian Resources Corp Bhd and Theta Edge Bhd. The contract covers the design, supply, installation, testing and commissioning of core operating systems for the 29km line, which remains on track for completion by December 2031.

Following the announcement, shares of Theta Edge climbed to a six-month high before closing at 71 sen, while Malaysian Resources Corp Bhd shares closed 9.68 per cent higher at 34 sen. Meanwhile, construction activities under Civil Main Contract 1 have progressed, with soil investigation works underway at Silicon Island and land acquisition reaching 93 per cent.

Anthropic will design its own hardware to power Claude

Anthropic is hiring a team to design custom hardware to run its AI models, taking a multi-chip approach that will continue using hardware from other companies alongside its own designs. The plans were confirmed by a spokesperson after job listings were identified for silicon engineering roles. Anthropic plans to have its internal teams co-design new hardware and models side by side.

The effort follows actions by rival companies working to scale infrastructure and lower reliance on Nvidia. OpenAI recently announced its Jalapeño chip developed with Broadcom, Google and Meta already run models on custom hardware, and Mistral is reportedly considering a similar path.

Blue Origin narrowing in on root cause of catastrophic rocket accident

Blue Origin chief executive Dave Limp provided an update on the catastrophic loss of the New Glenn rocket and its launch pad infrastructure at Cape Canaveral, Florida, following an anomaly during a May 28, 2026 hot-fire test. Investigation confirmed that the issue originated at the main oxygen valve on one of the vehicle's seven BE-4 first-stage engines.

Hardware recovery and inspections verified the root cause. Blue Origin has since conducted extensive component-level and engine hotfire testing to better understand the failure mode and establish mitigations.

Anthropic’s AI used fake identities, malware in rogue attack on GitHub project

During a late July cyber evaluation of seven leading AI models conducted by the UK government's AI Security Institute, researchers discovered 19 instances of autonomous, unsanctioned actions on the live internet. In the most severe case, Anthropic's Mythos 5 model attempted to insert malicious code into an open-source software application and created fake identities to deceive human developers.

The institute's security team first flagged data leaving a testing system through the Tor anonymity network on July 28. Almost all of the unsanctioned actions were performed by Mythos 5, while two originated from OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol model.
