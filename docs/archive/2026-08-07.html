ASEAN ANNIVERSARY

Southeast Asian Nations Mark 59th ASEAN Anniversary

*The region commemorated nearly six decades of cooperation alongside key diplomatic milestones.*

By the Dispatch Desk

Commemorating the 59th anniversary of ASEAN, regional leaders reflected on nearly six decades of effort to build a community of peace, stability, and shared prosperity. This year marked the first time ASEAN Day was celebrated with Timor-Leste as "ASEAN’s eleventh member state".

The event also marked the 50th anniversary of the "Treaty of Amity and Cooperation in Southeast Asia (TAC)", whose principles continue to foster trust and peaceful cooperation across the region. Over the past year, ASEAN continued to address shared challenges and advance common interests through close cooperation with external partners.

Looking ahead, efforts will focus on advancing the "ASEAN Community Vision 2045" by drawing strength from diversity, remaining united, and building a resilient region.

‘Maritime growth key to Brunei’s economic future’

Speaking at the launch of Brunei Darussalam Maritime Week 2026, the Minister of Transport and Infocommunications, Dato Mohd Riza DP Hj Mohd Yunos, stated that maritime development is "not simply a sectoral priority", but fundamental to economic growth and resilience. He noted that "The sea has always been the sultanate’s gateway to trade, food security, energy and international connectivity. Today, that gateway is more important than ever,".

Brunei has made investments in maritime infrastructure, including the expansion of Muara Port, which is scheduled for completion in the third quarter of 2027. The project is designed to more than double the annual container handling capacity of the port from 220,000 TEU to 500,000 TEU and will feature a 3.26-hectare Port Trade Zone.

Anson International, SPHI Marine sign MoU to explore maritime collaboration

Anson International Sdn Bhd and SPHI Marine Sdn Bhd have signed a Memorandum of Understanding at Dermaga Diraja as part of Brunei Darussalam Maritime Week 2026. Malaysia-China currency swap arrangement renewed for 5 years

Bank Negara Malaysia and the People's Bank of China have renewed their bilateral currency swap arrangement for a five-year term effective from 2026 to 2031. The size of the arrangement has been expanded to 220 billion renminbi (RM130 billion) from the previous 180 billion renminbi (RM110 billion).

The agreement was first established in 2009 and renewed in 2012, 2015, 2018 and 2021. The arrangement facilitates the use of local currencies for the settlement of bilateral trade and investment between Malaysia and China, strengthens economic and financial linkages, and contributes to regional financial stability and resilience.

Thai PM vows new gun law after deadly school shooting

Anthropic will design its own hardware to power Claude

Anthropic is hiring a custom silicon team to design chips to run its models, confirming its plans after job listings were noticed for semiconductor and silicon positions. A spokesperson confirmed that the company will maintain a multi-chip approach, using hardware from other companies alongside its own designs as it continues to scale up. Judge rules Meta caused "public nuisance" and must fund mental health treatment

A New Mexico judge, Bryan Biedscheid, ordered Meta to pay $567 million for a fund to alleviate a public nuisance created by its social media services by funding youth mental health care. This order follows a March 2026 jury trial that required Meta to pay $375 million in civil damages after finding that the company failed to effectively protect kids from child exploitation and misled parents about app safety.

The lawsuit was filed in Santa Fe County court in 2023 by New Mexico Attorney General Raul Torrez. Phase 1 of the case addressed Unfair Practices Act claims and civil penalties, while Phase 2 focused on the state's request for public nuisance abatement.
