Wildfires in Kalimantan Send Toxic Haze Across Southeast Asia

*Blazes have burned nearly 500,000 acres, worsening air quality and prompting calls for accountability.*

By the Dispatch Desk

Wildfires raging across Indonesia’s Kalimantan province have burned more than 202,000 hectares, sending smoke and toxic haze across Southeast Asia.

The fires, which have consumed nearly 500,000 acres, continue to worsen regional air quality and pose an active threat to wildlife.

In response to the expanding destruction and haze, environmental groups are calling for accountability.

Sabre Brunei Travel Fair 2026 to offer exclusive travel deals and promotions

Sabre Travel Network (Brunei) Sdn Bhd will host the 21st Sabre Brunei Travel Fair from 12 to 13 September 2026 at Hall 1, BRIDEX International Conference Centre, Jerudong. The event will feature more than 90 booths represented by 37 exhibitors, including 18 travel agencies, airlines, financial institutions, telecommunications providers, and insurers. Sabre will also deploy more than 70 workstations and laptops to support real-time booking management, while Acting General Manager Hj Mohd Shamiruddin Hj Sharbini highlighted the fair as a platform to stimulate market demand ahead of Visit Brunei Year 2027.

Royal Brunei Airlines will participate with promotional offers, including travel to Jeddah from June to July 2027 and a Year End London Special starting 25 December 2026, alongside grand draw prizes and Double Status Miles for Royal Skies members. The event will also feature tourism bodies from Brunei, Sabah, and Thailand, as well as various local insurance providers, commercial exhibitors, and food and beverage vendors.
