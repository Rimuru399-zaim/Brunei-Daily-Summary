108 schools in Serian closed as haze worsens

A total of 108 schools in Serian have been temporarily closed as a precautionary measure after air pollutant index readings in the area exceeded 200 amid worsening haze. The Sarawak disaster management committee stated that home-based teaching and learning would continue throughout the closure to ensure lessons are not disrupted.

Air pollutant index readings in the state have shown an upward trend since Aug 1, with Tebedu and Serian recording very unhealthy readings of 212 and 202, while eleven other areas recorded unhealthy readings. The committee advised the public to reduce outdoor activities and urged high-risk groups to limit their exposure to polluted air.

Technology

Terabytes of credentials leaked in massive supply-chain attack

A supply-chain attack on LiteLLM, an open source tool used to streamline software development, exposed terabytes of access credentials belonging to major entities including Microsoft, Amazon, Cisco, Samsung, and Salesforce. Security firms CloudSEK and Hudson Rock reported that exposed credentials included cloud keys, repository tokens, SSH keys, Kubernetes secrets, package publishing credentials, environment variables, and provider keys, which CloudSEK stated could grant access to over 2,500 organizations.

The credentials were compromised during a 40-minute window in March when users downloaded altered versions of LiteLLM from the official Python Package Index repository. Hudson Rock uncovered the compromised data through the analysis of a 195TB file, though neither security firm identified the source of the leak.

Google reveals 2026 hardware lineup: Pixel 11, Pixel Watch 5, and Pixel Tag

Google announced its 2026 hardware lineup, introducing the Pixel 11 series alongside the Pixel Watch 5 and its first in-house tracking device, the Pixel Tag. The Pixel 11 devices evolve the design introduced with the Pixel 9, adding minor hardware updates and additional Gemini-powered artificial intelligence capabilities. Orders for the new devices opened on the day of the announcement.

The Pro models of the Pixel 11 feature a notification light system called HiLight, which uses a ring of RGB LEDs inside the rear flash assembly. The ring illuminates when interacting with Gemini to show that the system is functioning without requiring screen viewing, functioning primarily when the phone rests face down. The base model Pixel does not include this feature.

Gemini becomes Google's fastest-growing product ever as it hits 1B users

Google CEO Sundar Pichai announced that Gemini has reached 1 billion monthly active users, making it the fastest product to reach that benchmark in the history of the company. While 13 other Google products have reached 1 billion users, Gemini completed the milestone faster than any predecessor.

The monthly active user metric counts individuals who open the Gemini application or access the Gemini web interface to submit prompts or use Gemini Live at least once a month. The figure does not include user encounters with Gemini features integrated into other products, such as email organization in Gmail, document summaries in Drive, or search features like AI Mode and AI Overviews.
