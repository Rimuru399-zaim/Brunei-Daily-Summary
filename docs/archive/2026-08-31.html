Indonesia forest fire pollutants could drift towards Malaysia, Singapore, Brunei

Smoke pollutants from forest and land fires in Sumatra and Kalimantan are drifting towards the Malacca Strait and Natuna Sea, raising the risk of transboundary haze in Malaysia, Singapore, and Brunei. A forecast from Indonesia's Meteorology, Climatology and Geophysics Agency indicated that winds could carry concentrations of PM2.5 northwest towards the neighboring nations. Air quality has reached hazardous levels in parts of Kalimantan, where visibility dropped to between 1 and 1.5 kilometres across three priority provinces.

Berton Panjaitan of the National Disaster Mitigation Agency reported that hotspots increased significantly in Central Kalimantan and West Kalimantan, while declining in South Kalimantan. A total of 48,667 personnel and 53 aircraft have been deployed across six priority provinces for monitoring and firefighting operations, with additional suppression efforts underway in West Papua.

Inside Meta’s push to put robots to work in data centers

Meta is testing robots to perform tasks such as resetting servers and plugging in cables within its data centers, according to current and former workers. The initiative aims to help the company run its growing data center infrastructure with fewer staff to restrain labor costs amid rising investments in artificial intelligence. The testing involves hardware from vendors including Kinova, ABB, and Watney Robotics.

Evaluations include testing a Kinova Gen3 robotic arm to cut power or power cycle servers, alongside another machine designed to exchange networking cables. One data center employee estimated that a successful deployment could take over as much as 80 percent of the workload for certain roles.

A 12TB Steam “teraleak” spills more than a decade of lost PC gaming history

A data leak encompassing over 12TB of files from Steam between 2003 and 2013 is circulating via a BitTorrent tracker. The collection contains thousands of depots from the platform's retired "Steam2" server architecture, covering public releases alongside previously unseen playtest, prototype, and pre-release builds from Valve and third-party publishers.

The archived material ends in 2013 because Valve transitioned from the proprietary format of "Steam2" to the standard HTTP file trees of the SteamPipe system. The shift removed approval bottlenecks on Valve's end and allowed patches and updates to download as file differentials.

NASA launches $4.3bn Roman Telescope to uncover universe’s hidden secrets

NASA launched the $4.3bn Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope aboard a SpaceX Falcon Heavy rocket from the Kennedy Space Center in Florida. The bus-sized observatory is traveling to Lagrange Point 2, located 1.6 million kilometres from Earth, to join the Webb Space Telescope in solar orbit. The baseline mission is scheduled for five years, with the possibility of a five-year extension if a robotic tanker becomes available to refuel the spacecraft.

Designed to investigate dark energy, dark matter, and cosmic expansion, the telescope's primary survey is set to cover more than 2 billion galaxies over more than a year. The observatory will also search for exoplanets beyond our solar system, with expectations of finding thousands of new worlds to assemble a statistical census of planetary systems.
