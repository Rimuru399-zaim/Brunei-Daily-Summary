NBT unveils the all-new Toyota Hilux in Brunei

NBT Toyota has introduced the Toyota Hilux 2.8 in Brunei, unveiling three variants: the Hilux Auto Mid, Auto High, and Manual Mid. The models were launched at The Atrium, The Mall Gadong, by executives from Inchcape Brunei, Baiduri Finance, and BIBD At-Tamwil Berhad, with showroom viewing scheduled from August 17. The vehicle features a 3,085mm wheelbase, a honeycomb front grille, LED headlamps, and a tailgate-assisted rear deck door.

The Hilux Auto Mid and Manual Mid are equipped with a 2.8-litre turbo diesel engine paired with either a six-speed automatic or six-speed manual transmission, alongside Multi-Terrain Select. Available interior features across the line-up include digital instrument clusters, infotainment screens, wireless smartphone connectivity, and dual-zone automatic air conditioning.

ASEAN

At least 47 dead in eastern Indonesia after magnitude 7.7 quake

At least 47 people have died after a magnitude 7.7 earthquake and dozens of aftershocks struck off eastern Indonesia on Saturday, according to the National Disaster Mitigation Agency (BNPB). Rescuers in the port town of Maumere recovered 20 bodies, while landslides, road blockages, and severed communications prevented emergency teams from reaching Nagekeo, the district situated closest to the epicentre. Minor tsunami waves measuring under one metre were recorded in several areas before authorities lifted an alert approximately three hours later.

Preliminary agency assessments recorded damage to hundreds of homes, 87 schools, 18 health facilities, and multiple office buildings, prompting about 2,000 residents in Nagekeo to evacuate. Tremors were felt across East Nusa Tenggara, West Nusa Tenggara, and parts of South Sulawesi, triggering power outages, road congestion, and structural collapses.

Sarawak wants transboundary haze issue tackled at Asean level

Sarawak Deputy Premier Datuk Amar Douglas Uggah Embas stated that transboundary haze affecting the state should continue to be addressed at the Asean level through the Sub-Regional Ministerial Steering Committee on Transboundary Haze Pollution. Malaysia's Natural Resources and Environmental Sustainability Ministry is set to present proposals through the committee, with Uggah noting that forest fires in locations such as Kuala Baram risk sending smoke into neighbouring Brunei.

Prevailing winds carrying smoke from hotspots in neighbouring regions have affected parts of Sarawak since Aug 8, driving Air Pollutant Index readings to very unhealthy levels in areas including Kuching, Samarahan, Sri Aman, Sarikei, Sibu, Bintulu, Samalaju, and Miri. In response to the haze and prolonged dry weather threatening local water sources, state authorities have applied to conduct cloud seeding operations and are evaluating the timing for their execution.

Vulnerability giving attackers full control of Macs is under active exploitation

Dutch officials at the Netherlands National Cyber Security Centrum have warned that a high-severity macOS vulnerability is under active exploitation, allowing attackers to execute malicious code. The agency observed that systems with port 5900 accessible from the Internet were targeted, with attackers gaining root access and installing a Monero crypto miner.

The vulnerability, tracked as CVE-2026-65400 and rated 7.1 out of 10 in severity, is caused by a flaw in the "state management" of the macOS screen sharing feature. Apple released a patch for the issue last week covering macOS Tahoe, Sequoia, and Sonoma.

First test flight of largest all-electric aircraft used just $5 of electricity

Heart Aerospace conducted the first test flight of its X1 demonstrator aircraft on August 12 at Plattsburgh International Airport in upstate New York. The flight of the battery-electric aircraft lasted nearly half an hour and used $5 of electricity.

Comparable in size to a small regional airliner, the X1 utilizes four wing-mounted electric motors, can exceed a maximum takeoff weight of 25,000 pounds, and generated more than one megawatt of power during the flight.
