ASEAN

Indonesia arrests 10 Myanmar nationals following liquid meth bust

Indonesian authorities have arrested 10 Myanmar nationals following the discovery of 2.6 metric tonnes of liquid methamphetamine aboard a ship sailing under a Tanzanian flag. A joint operation involving the drug agency, police, and customs intercepted the vessel on Monday, Aug 17, in the waters of the Riau Islands after tracking it since December. The illicit substance was found stored inside a water tank and eight 200-litre drums hidden on top of a container on the vessel.

National Narcotics Agency chief Suyudi Ario Seto called for a total ban on vapes, stating, "This is concrete evidence that narcotics crimes have evolved from solid forms into liquid forms to infiltrate the public lifestyle through the use of vapes or electronic cigarettes," according to authorities. Officials added that the suspects are linked to another ship intercepted in Riau waters earlier this month with 1.3 tonnes of ketamine, which was also crewed mostly by Myanmar nationals.

Chinese regulators tell Tesla to fix nearly 3 million cars

Tesla is facing a recall of 2.98 million Model 3 and Model Y electric vehicles in China as regulators take a stricter approach to safety concerning electronically controlled doors. Following crashes where occupants were trapped inside burning vehicles, the automaker must add interior warning labels to show how rear doors can be opened during an emergency that causes power loss.

Starting next year, new regulations will mandate specific handle dimensions for newly approved vehicles, though models already on sale are grandfathered until the end of 2028. To address cars currently on the road, Tesla must physically add the warning labels to vehicle interiors and deploy a software update that automatically lowers all windows if a collision occurs.
