Anwar to table 2027 Budget at 3.30pm today

Prime Minister Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim will table the 2027 Budget in the Dewan Rakyat at 3.30pm today, marking the fifth budget under his Madani Government. As the second budget under the 13th Malaysia Plan, it focuses on ten key priorities, including easing living costs, narrowing regional disparities, reinforcing the social safety net for gig workers, and preparing for an ageing population by 2030.

According to the Parliament portal calendar, the Supply (Budget) Bill 2027 will be debated at the policy stage for eight days starting next week. Ministerial winding-up speeches will run from Oct 26 to 29, followed by the committee stage debate between Nov 2 and 26 before the legislation is put to a vote.

Haze: 36 areas record unhealthy API readings, Seremban and Segamat highest

A total of 36 areas across the country recorded unhealthy Air Pollutant Index readings as of 10pm, with Seremban in Negri Sembilan and Segamat in Johor registering the highest levels at 188. Unhealthy air quality was also reported across various locations in the Klang Valley, the east coast, and northern regions.

The Malaysian Meteorological Department indicated that haze is expected to persist in several areas, with an absence of rain in parts of Johor expected to hinder near-term recovery. The department advised residents in affected regions to curtail outdoor physical activities, wear appropriate face masks when outdoors, and drink sufficient water to prevent related health complications.

RIP Margaret Hamilton, whose code saved the Apollo 11 Moon landing

Margaret Hamilton, who coined the term "software engineering" and led the development of onboard flight software for NASA's Apollo program, died last week at the age of 90. Hamilton received the Presidential Medal of Freedom in 2016 and was featured as a LEGO minifig the following year alongside astronauts Mae Jemison and Sally Ride.

Born in Paoli, Indiana, in 1936, Hamilton earned a mathematics degree from Earlham College in 1958. Olivier de Weck, interim head of the MIT Department of Aeronautics and Astronautics, described her as a fundamental contributor to the Apollo program who engineered advanced code and guided a team during the infancy of the software field.

Amazon builds 1,000th satellite, will launch space internet service by end of year

Amazon is preparing to launch a low-Earth orbit satellite constellation to provide broadband internet after nearly a decade of development. The company enters a sector dominated for the past five years by SpaceX's Starlink, while competitor OneWeb has offered only limited services.

Amazon's orbital internet project is being monitored by prospective customers, including shipping businesses, airlines, and governments. The broadband division is led by Rajeev Badyal, who previously directed Starlink during its initial years until Elon Musk dismissed him eight years ago.

Nvidia's big bet on physical AI aims for safer robotaxis, humanoid robots

Nvidia has directed billions of dollars into physical artificial intelligence technologies, focusing on robotics and autonomous vehicles alongside its data center business. To lessen the danger of machines injuring people nearby, the company introduced its Halos safety system in 2025 to establish software and hardware guardrails for self-driving vehicles.

In June 2026, the company extended the architecture with Nvidia Halos for Robotics to cover surgical machines, warehouse mobile units, and humanoid factory robots. Amit Goel, head of robotics ecosystem and edge computing at Nvidia, stated that the platform was introduced to address safety as the primary bottleneck facing increasingly capable artificial intelligence models and robotic hardware.
