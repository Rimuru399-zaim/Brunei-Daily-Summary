Intel gets waiver after worries fuzzy US ban on foreign-made robots may hit fabs

On October 22, the Federal Communications Commission published an order granting Intel a one-year waiver to continue using foreign-made robots in its semiconductor manufacturing facilities in the United States. Intel sought the waiver following confusion surrounding a federal ban on certain foreign-made robots, wanting to ensure it could keep using the vehicles in its chip fabs.

The machines in question are automated guided vehicles used to move materials and items across factory floors and warehouses. According to Intel, these units operate on tracks or predefined paths and often require operator oversight.
