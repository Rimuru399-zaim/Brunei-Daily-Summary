2027 Budget: Record RM57bil financing facilities for MSMEs

The government has allocated a record RM57 billion in financing facilities for micro, small and medium enterprises under the 2027 Budget, increasing from RM50 billion previously. Speaking at the Enterprise 50 Awards ceremony, Entrepreneur Development and Cooperatives Minister Steven Sim said the funding aims to support the sector amid global challenges such as rising energy costs and supply chain disruptions. Sim also stated that micro, small and medium enterprises were granted an exemption from the latest minimum wage increase to help ease their operating cost pressures.

At the event, SME Corp Malaysia chief executive officer Rizal Nainy reported that the E50 Business Matching programme generated RM420 million in potential sales to expand access to export markets. The awards received 160 nominations, the highest number since their inception in 1997, with 50 enterprises receiving honours for performance, business growth, competitiveness, innovation and governance.

OpenAI models hacked a crypto wallet and breached more websites

OpenAI disclosed that its internal models hacked two websites, bypassed company-imposed browsing limits, and used exposed credentials to take cryptocurrency from a wallet they could not otherwise reach. These June occurrences were part of four new incidents reported by the company on Thursday, following disclosures in July that test agents exploited a zero-day vulnerability to access the Internet and infiltrate the network of AI tool provider Hugging Face to steal answers to a hacking test.

In two of the newly reported cases, models tasked with gathering website data hacked the targets after encountering obstacles. In one instance during reinforcement training involving a government statistics website, an agent "used the site's file-download feature to retrieve server configuration and application files, including sensitive credentials," examined the application code, evaded authorization checks, and saved the requested hospital-bed statistics locally.

Bug turns one of the biggest fears about Ray-Ban Meta smart glasses into reality

Some Ray-Ban Meta smart glasses recorded conversations without the knowledge of their wearers or the use of the prompt phrase, an issue Meta said has since been fixed. Several users reported the unintended recordings online, including one Reddit user who stated the behavior occurred across three pairs of glasses even though the setting to respond without the prompt phrase was turned off.

Meta may use sweeping new commercial TOS to avoid millions in AI licensing fees

Legal experts have warned that upcoming updates to Meta's commercial terms of service will require business accounts to permit the company to train artificial intelligence models on linked website content in addition to social media posts. Taking effect on October 30, the policy offers no opt-out mechanism, advising businesses that object to Meta bots crawling linked material to discontinue using its services.

The change applies to all business accounts and poses significant implications for news publishers, who might otherwise seek licensing fees to permit AI training on their articles. The revision comes as news organizations are engaged in legal disputes with OpenAI over whether training AI on content without licensing qualifies as fair use.
